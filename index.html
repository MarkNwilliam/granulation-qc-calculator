Mathematical models and standards behind the recommendations
ModelEquationUsed for
Flow and compressibility are read through Carr and Hausner (USP <1174>). Die fill follows the Beverloo orifice flow law. Speed sets the dwell time t = (contact angle / 360) x (60 / rpm). Compact formation follows Heckel, Ryshkewitch-Duckworth and Leuenberger, which is where capping and lamination come from. Weight variation is held against the in process specification and USP <905>. All advisory, confirm on the first compression.
